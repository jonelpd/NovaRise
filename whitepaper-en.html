NVRA Whitepaper · Conceptual draft

Document updated · 7 November 2026 · Not an offer to sell tokens or a financial investment.

Status: NVRA issued on Base Mainnet. Contract: 0xa1128B1F4CB8394c179eb2558608a7302972EcBF. No public sale or announced liquidity.

Project vision

NovaRise Analytics aims to develop useful digital products, including games, mobile applications, analytical tools and Web3 services. These are plans, not launched NVRA products.

The role of NVRA

NVRA is intended as a utility token for optional access to eligible services and features, subject to technical feasibility and legal assessment. Basic public information should not require NVRA.

Network and official contract

NVRA is deployed on Base Mainnet (chain ID 8453), an Ethereum layer-2 network. Network fees are paid in ETH. Token contract: 0xa1128B1F4CB8394c179eb2558608a7302972EcBF. View on BaseScan.

Supply and issuance

NovaRise Utility Token (NVRA) was issued on 7 November 2026 as an ERC-20 with 18 decimals and a fixed supply of 1,000,000 NVRA, using two transactions: factory deployment and initialization. BaseScan shows 1,000,000 NVRA and five initial holders. The token contract has no subsequent mint function.

Initial allocation

30% games, mining and participation (300,000); 25% ecosystem and apps (250,000); 20% liquidity and expansion (200,000); 15% team vesting (150,000); 10% reserve (100,000). Allocation contracts were created, but their individual balances and permissions still require public documentation and verification. An allocation for liquidity does not mean trading liquidity has been supplied.

Security and governance

The design uses a 2-of-3 Safe controller and team vesting with a 365-day cliff followed by 1,095 days of linear release from actual initialization. Deployment proceeded without an independent audit. Public source verification, independent bytecode-reference comparison, operational controls and legal review remain pending. A Safe-controlled balance does not imply liquidity is locked.

Roadmap

Completed: 1,000,000 NVRA issued on Base Mainnet on 7 November 2026. Next: verify and publish factory, vault and vesting source code and addresses; document allocations; review security and legal compliance; build game and application utilities; define token use conditions. No public token sale or established market price.

Inclusive digital education

NovaRise adds accessible educational games and apps for children and people with disabilities as a strategic objective, including literacy, mathematics, communication, memory, attention, everyday skills and independence. Product design should involve education and accessibility specialists and, where appropriate, disabled people and families. Needs differ across users.

Design goals include adaptable interfaces, keyboard navigation, screen reader support, captions and text alternatives, plain language, sound and motion controls, personalized difficulty, privacy by design and usability testing. These are planned goals, not launched features.

Children will not need NVRA purchases, crypto wallets, transactions, financial advertising or token rewards to use educational experiences. Any potential NVRA utility for adults, institutions or supplementary services will be assessed separately, subject to legal review. Education plan.

Risks and compliance

Smart-contract defects, wallet compromise, service adoption, liquidity, operational costs and changing rules are material risks. An EU/Spain legal assessment remains pending. No financial return is promised.
