NVRA Whitepaper · Conceptual draft

Working document · 30 October 2026 · Not an offer to sell tokens or a financial investment.

Status: planned. No NVRA token has been issued on Base.

Project vision

NovaRise Analytics aims to develop useful digital products, including games, mobile applications, analytical tools and Web3 services. These are plans, not launched NVRA products.

The role of NVRA

NVRA is intended as a utility token for optional access to eligible services and features, subject to technical feasibility and legal assessment. Basic public information should not require NVRA.

Chosen network

The proposed production network is Base Mainnet (chain ID 8453), an Ethereum layer-2 network. Network fees are paid in ETH. No NVRA contract has been deployed there.

Supply and design

The planned maximum supply is 1,000,000 NVRA with 18 decimals, issued once to a verified multisignature treasury. The production contract and custody have not been finalized.

Proposed allocation

30% ecosystem and rewards; 25% treasury and development; 20% community and adoption; 15% team vesting; 10% potential future liquidity. All figures are provisional.

Security and governance

A production release requires an independently reviewed contract, verified Safe multisignature custody, defined beneficiaries and enforceable distribution controls. A Safe balance alone does not technically lock liquidity.

Roadmap

First build and validate real services. Then complete security, legal and operational requirements. Only after formal approval consider deployment and service integration.

Risks and compliance

Smart-contract defects, wallet compromise, service adoption, liquidity, operational costs and changing rules are material risks. An EU/Spain legal assessment remains pending. No financial return is promised.
