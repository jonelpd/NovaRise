NVRA Whitepaper · Conceptual draft

Working document updated · 6 November 2026 · Not an offer to sell tokens or a financial investment.

Status: planned. No NVRA token has been issued on Base.

Project vision

NovaRise Analytics aims to develop useful digital products, including games, mobile applications, analytical tools and Web3 services. These are plans, not launched NVRA products.

The role of NVRA

NVRA is intended as a utility token for optional access to eligible services and features, subject to technical feasibility and legal assessment. Basic public information should not require NVRA.

Chosen network

The proposed production network is Base Mainnet (chain ID 8453), an Ethereum layer-2 network. Network fees are paid in ETH. No NVRA contract has been deployed there.

Supply and design

The candidate design provides for a fixed 1,000,000 NVRA with 18 decimals, issued once through an atomic factory. Five planned allocations include separate team vesting. No production token has been deployed.

Proposed allocation

30% games, mining and participation (300,000); 25% ecosystem and applications (250,000); 20% liquidity and expansion (200,000); 15% team vesting (150,000); 10% reserve (100,000). Actual on-chain execution is pending.

Security and governance

The design uses a 2-of-3 Safe whose configuration was checked with read-only Base queries, and team vesting with a 365-day cliff and 1,095-day linear release. Independent audit, external bytecode-reference comparison and operational controls remain pending. A Safe balance does not technically lock liquidity.

Roadmap

Candidate factory and token contracts have passed local tests, and two-transaction Base deployment costs have been estimated. Next: reproduce and verify deployment bytecode in a trusted signing environment, review live fees, separately approve each transaction and verify the on-chain result. Audit and legal review remain outstanding.

Risks and compliance

Smart-contract defects, wallet compromise, service adoption, liquidity, operational costs and changing rules are material risks. An EU/Spain legal assessment remains pending. No financial return is promised.
