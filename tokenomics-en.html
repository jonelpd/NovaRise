NVRA Tokenomics

Working document updated · 6 November 2026 · Not an offer to sell tokens or a financial investment.

Status: planned. No NVRA token has been issued on Base.

Maximum supply

1,000,000 NVRA (planned), fixed supply, 18 decimals. No Base Mainnet token has been deployed.

Games, mining and participation — 30%

300,000 NVRA allocated to game utilities, rewards and participation. Distribution mechanisms are not live.

Ecosystem and applications — 25%

250,000 NVRA for developing and integrating ecosystem applications and services. Not distributed.

Liquidity and expansion — 20%

200,000 NVRA allocated for potential future liquidity and growth. An allocation is not evidence of supplied or locked liquidity.

Team — 15%

150,000 NVRA with a fixed beneficiary in the candidate contract, a 365-day cliff and 1,095 days of subsequent linear release. The actual schedule begins at successful initialization, which has not occurred.

Reserve — 10%

100,000 NVRA reserved for contingencies and future needs, subject to custody and governance decisions.

Launch conditions

The design uses a 2-of-3 Safe treasury whose configuration was checked on Base through read-only queries. Independent audit, external bytecode-reference comparison, legal review and actual deployment remain pending.

Launch readiness checklist

The following items are prerequisites, not completed milestones:

  • ERC-20 and atomic factory: implemented as candidates and tested locally; deployment and independent audit pending.
  • 2-of-3 Safe treasury: configuration queried on Base; independent bytecode-reference comparison pending.
  • Allocation and vesting: parameters encoded in the candidate; activation and on-chain verification pending.
  • Legal and regulatory review, including applicable EU requirements: pending.
  • Working utility and operational controls: to be demonstrated before launch.

Current status: preparing a Mainnet deployment; no NVRA creation transaction has been signed or sent and no public token sale exists.
