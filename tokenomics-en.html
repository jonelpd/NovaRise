NVRA tokenomics and development plan

NovaRise is studying a fixed supply of 1,000,000 NVRA for a possible future Polygon mainnet deployment. No mainnet token has been launched.

This page is a planning document, not a token sale, investment offer, price forecast, or guarantee of future value. Legal and security reviews are pending.

Existing testnet prototype

A separate experimental NVRA contract exists on Polygon Amoy (chain ID 80002) with an initial issuance of 100,000,000 test tokens. These tokens have no assigned monetary value and will not automatically become mainnet NVRA.

Development roadmap

  1. Define and publish utility, distribution, release schedules and governance
  2. Design multisignature treasury and on-chain vesting controls
  3. Develop ERC-20 mainnet candidate, Access Pass, Participation and Rewards contracts
  4. Automated tests, threat modelling and independent security review
  5. Legal review (including EU MiCA as applicable) and operational readiness
  6. Testnet validation, community review and only then a separate mainnet deployment decision

All percentages and schedules are provisional until formal approval. No mainnet deployment, sale or liquidity has been announced.
