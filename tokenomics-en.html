Development roadmap

  1. Define and publish utility, distribution, release schedules and governance
  2. Design multisignature treasury and on-chain vesting controls
  3. Develop ERC-20 mainnet candidate, Access Pass, Participation and Rewards contracts
  4. Automated tests, threat modelling and independent security review
  5. Legal review (including EU MiCA as applicable) and operational readiness
  6. Independent audit, legal review, custody verification and explicit mainnet deployment approval

All percentages and schedules are provisional until formal approval. No Base mainnet deployment, sale or liquidity has been announced.

Base Mainnet · confirmed architecture

Base (chain ID 8453) is the chosen production network; NVRA has not been deployed. Fixed total supply: 1,000,000 NVRA, 18 decimals. Allocation percentages and release schedules remain provisional. Planned applications include gaming, mobile tools, analytics, smart contracts and community participation. The Base portal is read-only and does not provide NVRA access. Gas fees on Base are paid in ETH. The old Polygon Amoy token is a separate experiment.

Independent security audit, 3-of-5 Safe signers, EU/Spain legal review and formal launch authorization remain prerequisites.
