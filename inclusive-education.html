NOVARISE · EDUCATION

Inclusive digital education

Accessible learning games and apps for different learning styles and paces.

Planned initiative. These educational products are not yet available.

Our goal

Create useful learning experiences for children and people with disabilities, respecting different needs, ages, abilities and learning preferences. This is a planned development area, not a launched service.

First planned products

1. Learn through play: literacy and mathematics with configurable levels. 2. Communicate and explore: vocabulary, pictograms and customizable multimodal supports. 3. Everyday skills: guided routines and independence activities. 4. Mind games: memory, attention and problem-solving without pressure or competitive rankings.

Accessibility and participation

Design targets include WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable, keyboard navigation, assistive technology compatibility, captions, plain language, audio alternatives, motion controls and sufficient contrast. Testing should involve users with diverse needs and education and accessibility professionals.

Child privacy and protection

Data minimization; no public child profiles, financial advertising, token purchases, wallet connections or crypto rewards for children. Content and consent mechanisms require legal and safety review before launch.

Relationship with NVRA

NVRA is already issued on Base Mainnet, but holding tokens will not be required for learning. Any future token use by adults or institutions will be optional, separate from children's learning experiences and subject to technical and legal review.

Delivery plan

Phase 1: research with educators, families and disabled people. Phase 2: accessible web prototype for literacy and maths. Phase 3: usability, accessibility and privacy testing. Phase 4: limited pilot and educational evaluation. Phase 5: more apps and modules. No launch date is committed.
